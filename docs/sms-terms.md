# ShearSimplicity Appointment Notifications — SMS Terms

1. ShearSimplicity Appointment Notifications sends transactional SMS messages from salons that use ShearSimplicity to their clients. Messages include appointment confirmations, reminders, reschedule notices, cancellations, and occasional service updates (such as a stylist running late) related to a specific appointment the recipient has booked.

2. You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text "STOP" to the phone number the messages were sent from. After you send the SMS message "STOP" to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, just sign up as you did the first time and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.

3. If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program you can reply with the keyword **HELP** for more assistance, or you can get help directly at support@shearsimp.app.

4. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. As always, **message and data rates may apply** for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. You will receive messages related to your appointments — frequency varies depending on how often you book. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider.

6. If you have any questions regarding privacy, please read our privacy policy: https://shearsimp.app/privacy
